The Crown Jewel of Youth Development

La Masia: Where Legends Are Raised

Established in 1979 inside an ancient Catalan country farmhouse built in 1702 next to Camp Nou after a proposal by Johan Cruyff, La Masia de Can Planes became the most famous football academy on Earth.

Every youth team from Under-8s to Barça Atlètic plays the exact same 4-3-3 positional possession system, prioritizing technical intelligence, rondo drills, quick one-touch passing, and humility.

Unprecedented Ballon d'Or History (2010)

In 2010, FC Barcelona's La Masia became the first and only youth academy in football history to occupy all three spots on the Ballon d'Or podium: 1st Lionel Messi, 2nd Andrés Iniesta, 3rd Xavi Hernández. And on November 25, 2012, Barça fielded a team composed of all 11 La Masia graduates simultaneously on the pitch!

Museum of Glory

The Blaugrana Trophy Cabinet

FC Barcelona is one of the most decorated clubs in world football history, renowned for winning every single competition possible in a single calendar year.

Football Immortality

2009 Sextuple & Two Historic Trebles (2009, 2015)

Under Pep Guardiola in 2009, FC Barcelona achieved the unprecedented Sextuple (winning all 6 trophies in one calendar year: La Liga, Copa del Rey, UEFA Champions League, Supercopa de España, UEFA Super Cup, and FIFA Club World Cup).

Six years later in 2015, under Luis Enrique and powered by the legendary MSN trident (Messi, Suárez, Neymar — who scored 122 goals in that season alone), Barça became the first club in European history to win the Continental Treble twice.

The Greatest Rivalry in Sports

Historic El Clásico Triumphs

Select a legendary match below to relive the nights when Barça conquered Real Madrid in unforgettable style.

Feb 17, 1974 • Bernabéu 0 - 5

Cruyff's Masterclass in Madrid

In his debut season, Johan Cruyff orchestrated a stunning 0-5 demolition at the Santiago Bernabéu, ending Barça's 14-year title drought and changing the club's mentality forever.

Scorers: Asensi (2), Cruyff, Juan Carlos, Sotil
May 2, 2009 • Bernabéu 2 - 6

Birth of the "False 9" Messi

Pep Guardiola surprised the world by deploying Lionel Messi as a "False 9". Xavi provided 4 assists, Carles Puyol kissed his Catalan captain's armband, and Barça scored six at the Bernabéu.

Scorers: Henry (2), Messi (2), Puyol, Piqué
Nov 29, 2010 • Camp Nou 5 - 0

"La Manita" of Tiki-Taka

Widely regarded as the single greatest display of Tiki-Taka football in history. Guardiola's Barça defeated José Mourinho's previously unbeaten Real Madrid 5-0 on the club's 111th birthday.

Scorers: Villa (2), Xavi, Pedro, Jeffrén
Oct 26, 2024 • Bernabéu 0 - 4

Hansi Flick & La Masia Statement

With 6 La Masia graduates in the lineup, Barça stunned the Bernabéu 0-4. Lamine Yamal (17) became the youngest scorer in El Clásico history alongside a brace from Lewandowski and a goal from Raphinha.

Scorers: Lewandowski (2), Lamine Yamal, Raphinha
